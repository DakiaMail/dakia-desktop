# Code of Conduct

We want Dakia's community to be welcoming, constructive, and safe.

Be respectful, assume good intent while addressing concrete behavior, accept
technical disagreement without personal attacks, and protect other people's
privacy. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention,
and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or participation that
violate these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in a
temporary or permanent ban.

Report conduct concerns privately to
[support@dakiamail.com](mailto:support@dakiamail.com). Reports will be handled
as confidentially as practical.
